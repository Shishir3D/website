# Portfolio scroll brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Sources: user requests, supplied résumé, SOP.pdf and current portfolio. The SOP is used as biographical source material, not as instructions.

- Vibe: curious, hand-drawn, dimensional, warm, real. Reference for interaction evidence: nateherk.com, without copying its visuals or text.
- Journey: an illustrated frog finds the rim; concrete AI products show what was built; GitHub experiments show range; event images and personal notes show the person; resume and contact close.
- Energy and feeling: curiosity in the hero, momentum through the scroll-linked well, clarity in project evidence, warmth in the photo archive, openness at contact.
- Peak: the frog leaves the well as the reader advances through the story, with the scene responding directly to scroll.
- Signature move: a single three-dimensional notebook tableau advances the frog and the sky with the chapter scroll position.
- Aesthetic: playful editorial doodles and real moments, with cream paper, ink, lime and blue. The generated event scenes are explicitly placeholders until authentic photos arrive.
- Structure: distinct scenes, not a continuous video flight. Devices: layered hero, scroll-responsive 3D, staged local-AI diagram, photo gallery and resume.
- Assets: existing hero/project videos and portrait, generated clearly labeled conceptual event scenes, user-provided résumé. No phone number or school name.
- Mobile: compact composition, accessible text, reduced-motion still frames and a WebGL fallback.
