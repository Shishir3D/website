# Photo archive uploads

Add your real event photos here as JPG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF. They appear automatically in the Field Notes gallery after the site rebuilds. Use descriptive filenames such as `apiga-nepal-panel.webp` or `nasa-space-apps-team.jpg`; the filename becomes the title.

The three files ending in `-placeholder.webp` are AI-generated illustrative scenes. Replace them with your own event photos when available, then remove the corresponding placeholder file and update the curated captions in `src/components/PhotoArchive.tsx`. Do not present generated scenes as documentary photographs.
